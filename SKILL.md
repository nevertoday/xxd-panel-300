## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one 1-bit seal, not a grayscale photo and not a pixel illustration. Use only black and white, error-diffusion dither and density, with no gray ramp and no gradient. Let the figure emerge from absence. Edges may decay. Keep the seal small and quiet on a large field. Type is a tiny low-resolution archive index. Reject a full scene, a game sprite, a film-grain filter, a commercial logo template and decorative pattern.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-300/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
